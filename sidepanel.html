About Stella
Stella AI

Your persistent AI companion for Chrome.

Stella is a browser sidebar chatbot that lets you chat with the world's leading AI models — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Mistral — without leaving the page you're on. Keep conversations alive across tabs, manage browser context, and run multi-model comparisons, all in one place.

  • Multi-provider support — GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, Mistral
  • Persistent chat history across sessions
  • Page & tab context injection
  • Multi-agent side-by-side comparison
  • Slash commands & tab picker
  • Your API keys stay local — never shared
